Readiness Score Formula

Readiness = 0.35*Skills + 0.20*Assessments + 0.15*Career + 0.15*Market + 0.10*Projects + 0.05*SoftSkills
  • 35% Core Skills: Jaccard-weighted overlap of verified skills.
  • 20% Assessments: In-app diagnostic tests & verified LeetCode problems.
  • 15% Market Alignment: Frequency match across active database job requisitions.

Prioritized Learning Gap Formula

Priority = SkillGap × IndustryDemand(1-5) × RoleImportance(1-5)
  • 6-Factor Job Fit: 50% Skills, 20% Role, 10% Exp, 10% Location, 5% Mode, 5% Salary.
  • Demand Forecaster: Flags Critical Shortage (>30% growth, >40% deficit).

/api/students GET, POST, PUT Profile management, verified badges, roadmap milestones, AI situation guide.
/api/assessments GET, POST Diagnostic quiz questions, submission evaluation, LeetCode verification.
/api/jobs GET, POST Role filtering, vacancy posting, multi-factor candidate match scoring.
/api/employers GET, POST Organizational profile, AI-ranked applicant pool, hiring metrics.
/api/institutes GET, POST Curriculum gap matrix, batch placement readiness, bridge training cohorts.
/api/government GET, POST Regional deficit heatmap, 12-to-24 month forecast, policy subsidy allocations.
/api/intelligence GET Unified cross-portal alignment pipeline, National Employability Index (76.4%).
/api/events GET (SSE) Real-time Server-Sent Events stream for instant cross-tab live updates.
/api/health GET PostgreSQL connectivity, memory usage, uptime, service diagnostic probe.
